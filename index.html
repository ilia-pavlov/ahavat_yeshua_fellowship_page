Special Events

Christmas Gifts for Kids in Samarkand

We're collecting New Year gifts for 70 children at a church in Samarkand, Uzbekistan 🇺🇿. The local pastor's church will pack them into gift bags and hand them out — and we'll show the photos and video at our service.

70 kids: 52 school-age (27 girls, 25 boys) and 18 ages 3–6 (11 girls, 7 boys) Bring to Ahavat Yeshua Fellowship
How to help & what to bring

Pick a way to help:

  1. Buy one item for many kids — e.g. 52 notebooks, 52 pencil sets, or a few backpacks
  2. Take on 2–3 children and put together a set for their age
  3. Bring money if you're not sure what to buy — we'll do the shopping

It doesn't have to be new! Bring things from home in good condition.

✅ What to bring:

  • School supplies: scissors, pencils, erasers, sharpeners, pens, markers, notebooks, sketchbooks, play-dough, pencil cases, backpacks
  • Russian-language books and coloring books
  • Balls (soccer, volleyball, small balls for little ones), Rubik's cubes, badminton
  • Stuffed animals 🧸
  • For girls: bracelet kits, hair ties, hair clips, brushes
  • For boys: trucks, dinosaurs, superhero toys
  • Puzzles and LEGO

❌ Please don't bring:

  • Candy or sweets
  • Clothes or shoes
